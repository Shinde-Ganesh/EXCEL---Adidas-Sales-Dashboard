# EXCEL---Adidas-Sales-Dashboard
Excel Dashboard from Raw Data for ADIDAS Sales

An analysis of the Adidas Sales Dashboard - USA reveals key highlights across sales trends, retailer performance, regional distribution, and sales channels:

**1. Monthly Sales Trend (2020 – 2021)**
   
  2020 Performance: Sales remained relatively stable and lower throughout 2020, hovering between roughly $1.08M and $2.46M per month, dipping to a low near $882K around June 2020.
  
  2021 Growth Surge: Sales saw a dramatic increase starting in early 2021, climbing rapidly from $5.52M in January 2021 to peak at $7.83M in July 2021, with   another strong peak of $7.78M in December 2021.

**2. Retailer Performance (Sales & Operating Profit)**

  Top Retailers: West Gear generated the highest total sales (nearing $24M), followed closely by Foot Locker and Sports Direct.
  Lower Volume Retailers: Walmart, Amazon, and Kohl's recorded lower overall sales volumes compared to specialized sporting goods retailers.
  Operating Profit: Operating profit closely mirrored total sales volume across all retailers, with West Gear and Foot Locker driving the largest share of              profitability.

**3. Geographical Distribution**
   
  High-Performing States: West Gear territory states like New York ($6.42M high indicator range) and Texas (~$4.64M) show substantial total sales.
  Regional Coverage: Sales are spread across all major US regions (West, Midwest, South, Southeast, and Northeast), with strong concentrations along the West        Coast, Texas, and the Northeast corridor.

**4. Sales Method Share**

  In-Store (40%): Remains the primary channel for generating sales volume.
  Outlet (33%): Represents the second-largest share of total sales.
  Online (27%): Accounts for over a quarter of total revenue, showing strong e-commerce adoption.
